Zigarot headed into the Screen Queensland offices to meet the Games Residency cohort in October 2026, and met some epic game developers! The Games Residency program is a Queensland, Australia exclusive system that runs a new cohort each year, helping to support developers to level up their game. You'll find some links below to some of the games and artists we saw, or check out the VOD on twitch (soon to be on youtube).

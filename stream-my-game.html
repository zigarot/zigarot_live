Looking for coverage for your game? Fill out this form and I'll get back to you if it's a fit. Genres I typically stream: [puzzle, automation, action, horror, immersive sim, strategy].

Generally, I will stream a game once for at least 3 hours. If the game is engaging, I will create shortform content and might continue to stream it in later sessions. If the game is not for me, I will let you know within 48hrs. If I know someone who will fit the game better, I will suggest the game to them, and them to you.

Coverage like this is free, and reliant on my schedule. If you are happy to pay for a stream (which I will prioritise if the game is suitable), there is an option to indicate this at the end of the form. My rates are available on my media kit.
